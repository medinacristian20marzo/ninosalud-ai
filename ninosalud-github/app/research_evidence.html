5,000 resamples of 24 province-year sequences. The interval crosses zero; it is not an equivalence test or a prediction interval for future patients.

The evaluation years were inspected during development. Provinces and years may be correlated. No operational forecasting advantage has been demonstrated.

What can be reproduced?

The matched EDA models, metrics, bootstrap and descriptive reference curves run from bundled aggregate inputs. The core EDA input was rebuilt from the participant-supplied source workbook and matched all 3,344 rows. Regional date auditing requires the separately obtained case-level source, which is not distributed.

Map geometry and older contextual views remain archived source-derived extracts; a complete raw-file-to-every-screen pipeline is not claimed.
