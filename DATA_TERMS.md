# Data and third-party material

The MIT license applies only to original project code and original explanatory text to the extent the project author can license them. It does not apply to upstream data, government documents, source screenshots or third-party names/trademarks.

The repository distributes aggregate research tables and source-derived contextual payloads with attribution. It does not distribute raw individual dengue records or claim ownership of the public authorities' data. The relevant source portals and original snapshot hashes are listed in `docs/DATA_SOURCES.md` and `data/SOURCE_FILE_HASHES.json`.

Upstream permission notices were not independently re-audited for every portal in this release. Do not infer an unrestricted data license from the repository's code license. Check source-specific conditions before further redistribution or commercial reuse. Official agencies have not endorsed this project. No official institution logo is used as the project's logo.
